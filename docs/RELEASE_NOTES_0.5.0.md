Filen Files 0.5.0

**New: share with your partner from Stack or Ami Pro.** Stack 0.21.0 and Ami Pro 0.7.0 need this version of Filen Files for sharing.
- New: share a folder or file with your partner from Stack or Ami Pro. Filen Files shows a confirm sheet first, so nothing is shared until you say yes.
- New: partner setup. Choose your partner once; they accept the request in Filen Files.
- New: Stack can show "Shared with me" and "Shared by me". Only shares with your partner are listed.
- Better: shared files open after Filen Files restarts, and offline if the folder was listed recently.
- Safety: only Stack and Ami Pro can use this, and each is checked by its signing certificate. Their access is removed when you sign out or switch to another Filen account, and they ask again at the next partner setup. The partner screens are protected against overlay tricks.
- Removed: the old QR pairing test code (it was never released).

Install: download Filen-Files.apk on an arm64 Android 8.0+ phone and open it. Updates install over the previous release.
